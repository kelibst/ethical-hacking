## ⚖️ Legal & Ethical Reminder

> **Only test on systems you have explicit, written permission to test.**
> All practice in this course is performed against **intentionally vulnerable** systems
> (Metasploitable 2, DVWA, TryHackMe, HackTheBox) or your own controlled lab environment.
> Unauthorized hacking is illegal under the Computer Misuse Act (UK), CFAA (US), and equivalents worldwide.
